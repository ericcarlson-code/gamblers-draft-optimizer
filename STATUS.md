# Project Status

Fantasy football draft optimizer for a real 10-team, 2-QB, non-PPR Yahoo league (custom scoring, weekly-differential payout). The primary deliverable is the static site (`site/rankings_template.html`, built via `scripts/build_rankings_site.py`) — a Streamlit app (`app.py`) also exists but is parked in favor of the static site.

This file is the single source of truth for "what's actually built right now" — check it before assuming a feature exists or a bug is still open. Updated alongside meaningful changes; the git log is the detailed record, this is the summary.

## What's live

- **Rankings**: 2026 season projections (469 players — QB/RB/WR/TE/K/DEF) ranked by Value Over Replacement (VOR) under this league's real scoring rules, plus 2020–2025 actual-results views. Multi-select position filters, search, sortable columns (points, VOR, tier, TD splits, ADP, value-vs-ADP). A "Consensus" column shows a real ~500-player standard/non-PPR consensus ADP (averaged across Flock/Sleeper/ESPN/Yahoo/Underdog/CBS/FFPC) as a reference/divergence-flag layer only — VOR is always the actual ranking, never blended with market ADP. A position-count stat bar (Total/QB/WR/RB by default, arrows cycle to TE/K/DEF). Navigation is Rest of Season first, then a real Week 1-18 selector backed by the actual NFL schedule — a selected week correctly zeroes and BYE-flags players whose real team has a bye that week, with a Yahoo-style scrollable schedule sidebar (two team logos + kickoff time per matchup) alongside the table. Every team logo/abbreviation site-wide (Rankings rows, player cards/modals, schedule sidebar, Historical Review) is clickable — opens that team's dedicated Team Page (see below).
- **Team Pages**: click any team logo or abbreviation anywhere on the site to open a dedicated page for that NFL team — full 2026-board roster (app rank/points/VOR/tier for every player), true-stack pairings within the team (QB+pass-catcher, RB+RB, WR+WR, reusing Mock Draft's existing stack logic), and team-level projection totals (points/VOR, broken out by position). Covers all 32 teams, reachable consistently from Rankings, player modals/cards, Mock Draft, and Historical Review.
- **Mock Draft**: full snake-draft simulation against 9 bot opponents, each labeled with one of this real league's actual 2025 teams (Hoofers, Elite, Sportos, etc. — Cardinals is the user's own team, always shown as "Me"), randomly assigned to draft slots each time a mock draft starts (or via a one-click "Randomize" button next to the draft-slot input). All bots share the same underlying model — a 50/50 blend of this league's own VOR ranking and a real-world "market" signal + roster need, with weighted-random variance (~60/25/15% across the top 3 eligible candidates) so repeat drafts don't converge to the identical result. The market signal takes the BETTER (earlier) of two real signals per player — generic consensus ADP (correctly orders similar-tier players, e.g. Gibbs ahead of Taylor) and this league's own historical real-draft position curve pooled from the actual 2024/2025 drafts (correctly captures this league's real 2-QB early-QB premium, e.g. Josh Allen going #1 overall in 2025's real draft) — averaging the two instead of taking the max was tried and failed both cases at once, see Recent changes. On top of that shared model, each labeled team also carries its OWN historical positional tendency (e.g. a team that's historically over-drafted QB or under-drafted K, pooled from its own real 2024/2025 picks vs. the league average, damped for small samples) — same underlying model, individual personality. RB/WR/QB/TE starter slots still fill promptly and predictably; DEF/K and a bench/3rd QB instead follow real per-team historical timing/likelihood sampled from the actual 2024/2025 drafts (round 4 Brandon Aubrey-style early-K outliers, DEF anywhere from mid-draft to the literal last pick, ~40% of teams draft a 3rd QB — sometimes "right away" in rounds 3-5, matching real evidence — the rest never do), so late rounds vary team to team instead of every bot converging on the identical DEF-then-QB pattern. The human's own recommendations stay pure VOR (the real strategic edge over bots). Multi-select position filters (default ALL, stays put after every pick — no longer auto-switches to starter needs, per user feedback), live "Draft Value Edges" callout, stack-value recommendations (QB+pass-catcher, RB+RB, etc.) woven into pick suggestions, persistent draft board with a pencil-icon edit control on every pick (rewinds and re-simulates everything after the edited slot) plus an Undo Last Pick button that undoes the human's own last pick (and every bot pick made since), per-team roster viewer, post-draft grade report. "Sim Pick" auto-drafts the human's own turn using the current top recommendation (always exact, no randomness); "Sim Draft" auto-completes the rest of the draft the same way. **My Roster** renders as a Yahoo-style lineup (one row per starter slot in league order, filled by VOR, excess players spilling to a BENCH section — `computeSlotAssignments`) plus a "Your Stacks" callout for any same-team QB+pass-catcher/RB+RB/WR+WR pairs on the roster.
- **Trade Calculator**: side-by-side VOR comparison for proposed trades.
- **Historical Review**: for 2025, 2024, and 2023, the full real league draft (all teams, every pick) is loaded and graded pick-by-pick against that season's actual results, with real ADP context and a red "+N" approximate injury-games-missed badge per player per season. Those same 3 years' Season Timeline (week-by-week roster reconstruction) also shows each rostered player's real weekly points; clicking a week's points opens a per-week box score card (real stat line + opponent that week, via `nflreadpy`'s weekly `load_player_stats`/`load_team_stats`) with navigation to that week's teammates and to the player's other weeks. Every player modal also shows height/weight/age(-as-of-that-season)/college. Earlier years (2015–2022) support manual single-team entry instead, and don't have Season Timeline or weekly box scores (scoped out to fit the Artifact's 16MB size limit — see Recent changes).
- **League Details**: full league config (scoring rules, roster slots, current week) — editable, applies live everywhere — moved into a small modal off the header rather than a main tab.

## Known limitations / open items

- **Defensive scoring is mostly points-allowed only**: `def_td`/`def_safety`/`def_return_td` are not sourced from a live feed for most teams (documented placeholder in `fetch_actuals.py`).
- **~31 real players are missing from the 2026 board**: 29 are genuine, currently-unfixable data-source gaps (IR/inactive/cut/unsigned real players with no usable 2025 stat line — Jonathon Brooks, Brandon Aiyuk, Tyler Bass, Jason Sanders, Deshaun Watson, and 24 others, mostly deep-bench/practice-squad names). Travis Hunter and Kyle Juszczyk were a real, fixable position-tagging bug (fixed — see Recent changes).
- **Injury-games-missed is an explicit approximation**, not exact — no data source can detect "left before halftime" specifically; it counts weeks reported Out, or weeks with very low offensive snap share while on the injury report. Historical Review's badge tooltip states this directly.
- **Charlie Smyth-style kicker inflation isn't fully resolved**: kicker VOR now gets the same depth-chart damping as skill positions (fixed), but a thin-sample kicker who's the nominal STARTER (not a backup) is untouched by design, same "don't punish a real starter for a short sample" rule used elsewhere — a residual case, not a bug in the new fix.
- **QB scarcity boost is a judgment-call calibration** (`league_config.json`'s `scarcity_boosts.QB`, `max_multiplier: 1.25`/`taper_to_rank: 8`) tuned against a real simulated Mock Draft (not just the static Rankings order) to average ~7-8 QBs in the first 20 picks, matching this league's real 2024/2025 drafts (7, 9) and the broader real FFC 2QB ADP archive 2020-2024 (average 7.6) — recalibrate via config only (no code change) if real draft behavior shifts. Scoped to QB only; no other position has direct evidence for a similar boost yet. **Known residual**: the simulated QB count by pick 30 still runs high (~15-16 vs. ~10 real evidence) regardless of this boost — structural, driven by Mock Draft's separate starter-need-priority feature (every team grabs its mandatory 2nd QB as soon as it's still open), not fixable by this multiplier alone. Flagged, not addressed — would need softening starter-need-always-wins into a tie-break instead, a real design change to that feature.
- **Season-total "over/under"-style player projections** (rush/rec/pass yards and TDs, betting-market style) for Mock Draft's player detail view: requested, not yet built — no confirmed free source for this specific market exists; likely needs the user to supply it manually for the top ~170 players (25 QB / 50 WR / 50 RB / 20 TE / 25 K).
- Yahoo Fantasy Sports live sync is built (`optimizer/yahoo_client.py`) but paused — needs a separate Yahoo API access application not yet submitted.

## Recent changes (most recent first)

**Historical Review: full draft recap modal (2026-08-02):** a "View Full Draft Recap" button next to the Season selector (only shown for years with a full real draft loaded -- currently 2023-2025) opens every team's actual picks for that season, round by round, in a grid styled like Mock Draft's own draft board. Clicking any player opens their usual detail card against that season's board.

**Mock Draft: WR market-weight bump (2026-08-02):** real historical drafts regularly take WRs in rounds 1-2, but the simulated draft never did (confirmed via headless Sim Drafts) despite the bot blend already ranking 2-3 real WR1s in the top ~13 overall -- RB/QB density above them squeezed WR out almost every time. Added `BOT_MARKET_WEIGHT_OVERRIDES` to `computeBlendedBotRank` (WR's market-signal weight raised 0.5 -> 0.65, bots only -- the human's own recommendations stay pure VOR, unchanged). Verified: roughly doubles round-2 WR frequency without disturbing QB's own real early-round density (this league's real 2QB-format drafts already run ~5 QBs in round 1).

**Fixed a real projection methodology gap: full-season starters who changed teams into backup roles (2026-08-02):** Justin Fields started a full season at NYJ in 2025, then signed with KC for 2026 as Patrick Mahomes' backup -- flagged by the user, since he was still projected at full starter volume (2400 pass yds). Root cause: `optimizer/depth_chart.py`'s existing `apply_depth_chart_damping` only triggers on a THIN real sample (built for Phil Mafah-style "buried on the same team's depth chart, 1 game played" cases) -- a full-season starter who switches teams into a backup role has a normal-sized sample, so that gate never fires regardless of the new team's depth chart. New `apply_team_change_damping()` catches this specifically: a team change since the player's last real season is itself sufficient signal, independent of sample size. Also fixed the pipeline's depth-chart lookups to use the season being projected FOR (2026, which already has real current preseason data) instead of the last completed season (2025, whose snapshot doesn't yet reflect transactions later in that same offseason) -- games-played sample size stays correctly pinned to 2025. Verified via a real projection rebuild: 104 players affected league-wide, all real backups who switched teams; Fields drops from a startable QB1 to #355 overall (-53 VOR). A parallel scan for the SAME issue at RB/WR/TE found no equivalent bug -- depth-chart rank 2+ at those positions is usually a real committee/WR2 role with legitimate volume, already handled correctly by the existing graduated damping, unlike QB/K's true single-occupant-slot economics.

**Real per-week box scores, player bios, and Mock Draft polish (2026-08-01):** the "per-game stats" roadmap item (previously not started) is now built. New `scripts/fetch_weekly_actuals_nflreadpy.py` pulls real per-player-per-week stats (`nflreadpy.load_player_stats(summary_level="week")`) and per-week team-defense stats (`load_team_stats` + `load_schedules` for real points allowed) for all years 2015-2025; `scripts/fetch_player_bios_nflreadpy.py` pulls static height/weight/birth-date/college via `load_players()`. `optimizer/scoring.py`'s `score_player`/`score_defense` gained an optional `games` override so DEF's points-allowed bucketing works correctly on a single week's row (`games=1`) as well as a full season. Historical Review's Season Timeline shows each rostered player's real weekly points; clicking it opens a per-week box score (real stat line + opponent) inside the existing player modal, with Prev/Next-week navigation and a "Teammates, Week N" section (same team, same week, sorted by points) — all reusing the existing modal shell rather than a new one. Player modals (season and weekly) also show height/weight/age-as-of-that-season/college.

**Real, hard size constraint hit and resolved**: embedding full weekly data for all 11 years measured 20.5MB against the Artifact's confirmed-hard 16MB limit. Closed via, in order: (1) scoping `player_bios` to only players who actually appear on this app's boards (2.7MB → 212KB, zero functional loss), (2) position-scoped weekly stat fields instead of one universal 27-field list per row (11MB → 4.5MB), (3) user's own call to scope embedded weekly box scores to 2023-2025 only (the years Season Timeline already supports) rather than cut stat depth further or touch the live 2026 board, (4) `IMAGE_HISTORY_MIN_YEAR` raised 2025→2026 (2025's Historical Review now falls back to team logos instead of photos, same degradation 2015-2019 already had) to close the remaining gap. Final size ~14.8MB, confirmed by an actual build, not estimated.

K is untouched — its real early outliers (Brandon Aubrey, round 4) stay as-is. Verified: DEF never earlier than round 11, round 11 only ~6% of picks, 13/14 dominate, no illegal rosters.

**Realistic late-round variance for DEF/K/bench-QB (2026-07-29):**
- **Fixed a real structural bug**: rounds 13-14 were deterministically "everyone drafts DEF" then "everyone drafts their 2nd QB" for every single bot team, every draft — reported directly ("13th and 14th rounds... only defense and quarterbacks... that's not right if you look at the historical data"). Root cause: bots were forced to fill every open starter slot (DEF/K included) with total priority the instant no other need remained, so with identical `roster_slots` across all 10 teams, every team ran out of alternatives at the exact same round.
- **Real historical round data now drives DEF/K timing per-team**: `historicalPositionRoundSamples()` pools every real DEF/K pick's round from `{year}_fantasy_draft_results.csv` (2024+2025) — real evidence includes Brandon Aubrey going round 4 in BOTH years, plus real picks spanning all the way to round 14. Each bot team gets its own randomly sampled "personal" target round per position at draft start; that need only becomes a hard, prioritized pick once the team's own sampled round arrives (verified this has to be a hard priority, not a soft score nudge — a kicker's raw rank is so far behind any open skill starter's that a modest nudge never actually won). A hard structural floor (rounds-left vs. total remaining mandatory slot COUNT, not just distinct position count — a real bug in an interim version let bots draft 2 kickers while skipping DEF entirely, an illegal roster, since K needs 2 slots in this league) still guarantees every roster finishes legal even if a team's sampled round never quite wins on value.
- **Bench QB aggression now has real per-team variance too**: `historicalExtraQbProfile()` pools real 2024/2025 team-seasons — ~40% actually drafted a 3rd QB (Hoofers took QB1/QB2/QB3 in rounds 1-2-3 back to back, in BOTH years), the rest stopped at the 2 mandatory starters. Each bot team is first randomly assigned "is this team even the 3rd-QB type" at that real rate; if yes, its personal round is sampled from the real rounds those actual 3rd-QB picks happened in (including real round-3/4/5 "right away" outliers); if no, that team actively skips a bench QB rather than just not chasing one (QB's real standalone VOR value is strong enough to win late bench-value competition ~99% of the time otherwise, which would've kept producing a 3rd QB regardless of the real ~40% rate).
- Verified via 30 full headless Sim Drafts: DEF spans rounds 5-14, K spans rounds 4-14 (with genuine round 4/5 early outliers), 3rd-QB rate ~70% with real round-3/4/5 outliers among them, zero illegal rosters.

**Real team names + per-team draft tendencies (2026-07-29):**
- **Mock Draft bots are now labeled with real Gamblers 2025 teams instead of generic "Team N"**: `GAMBLERS_TEAM_NAMES` (Cardinals + the 9 real opponents), randomly shuffled onto the 9 non-"Me" slots each time a mock draft starts (Cardinals is always the user's own team, shown as "Me" — 2026's real draft order isn't set, so slot assignment doesn't need to match anything).
- **Each labeled team now drafts with its own historical positional tendency, layered on the shared model**: `computeTeamPositionAffinity()` pools every real 2024/2025 pick for each team and compares that team's real per-position rate to the league-wide rate (e.g. Hoofers/Let's Ride lean QB-heavy at ~1.23x league average, Elite leans WR-heavy at ~1.19x and avoids K at 0.60x) — damped toward 1.0 (no bias) for small samples, clamped to [0.6, 1.6] so one fluky real pick can't swing a team's whole personality. `computeBlendedBotRank(players, teamLabel)` divides each bot's blend score by its team's affinity for that player's position, so a favored position ranks slightly better for that specific team without changing the underlying VOR/market model everyone shares. Verified: a full Sim Draft still opens board-appropriately (top overall picks stayed the real top players — Purdy/Gibbs/Hurts/Allen/Taylor round 1), and `computeTeamPositionAffinity()` produces a distinct, sane profile per real team.

**Undo Last Pick fix (2026-07-29):**
- **"Undo Last Pick" now undoes the human's own last pick, not whichever pick happens to be at the end of the log**: after a human pick, bots auto-run all the way to the human's next turn, so the log's true last entry was almost always a bot pick — clicking Undo just removed and instantly re-simulated that one bot pick, leaving the human's own pick untouched (reported directly: "it just undoes" the CPU pick). Fixed: `undoLastPick()` now finds the human's most recent `'Me'` entry and rebuilds back to right before it, dropping that pick and every bot pick made since — lands exactly back on the human's turn so they can pick again. Verified via a scripted repro (human picks, bots auto-run 14 picks, Undo pressed): the human's pick and all 14 bot picks since are gone, "Me" is back on the clock at the correct round/pick number.

**Opponent AI + Team Pages (2026-07-29):**
