Team Power Rankings

TeamTotal VORPlayers

Enter one of your own past drafts (which player you took each round) and grade it against what actually happened that season, under this league's current scoring. Good for spotting a specific mistake pattern -- e.g. an early QB who busted, or a cheap late pick who massively outperformed.

Scope limit: this only has your own picks, not the full 10-team draft, so it can't tell you the exact best alternative pick at your slot the way Mock Draft's grade report can (that needs the full simulated draft). Instead it compares each pick's actual result to what a typical pick in that round should return (and, where historical ADP data is available, to that round's real market expectation too), and separately surfaces the highest-value players you didn't draft at all that season.

    Real weekly matchup scores for this specific league — either synced from Yahoo (pending API access approval) or entered by hand week to week. Yahoo's own scoring sometimes comes out slightly different from this league's actual rules, so the plan is to pull the underlying raw stats and score them ourselves here, not just copy Yahoo's number.     Points and VOR reflect the league's current custom scoring config at time of generation. Tier = statistically significant value drop-off within a position (lower is better). In the Trend column, the solid line is actual results (2020–2025); the dashed segment and hollow dot are the 2026 projection, not an observed result.
